# Brain artwork attribution

The hero brain anatomy (`salpa-brain.svg`) is a recolored derivative of:

- **File:Human-brain.SVG** — lateral view of a human brain with cerebellum
  and brain stem, by **Jennifer Lange**,
  licensed **CC BY-SA 3.0**
  (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Human-brain.SVG).

Modifications for SALPA: flattened to a dark cinematic palette (charcoal
tissue, faint ivory sulci strokes), slimmed one 4px vessel path to a 1px
sulcus line. No anatomical paths were reshaped.

The sulci light-path data (`components/marketing/sulci-paths.ts`) is
sampled from the same file's cerebrum boundary polylines.

CC BY-SA 3.0 attribution and share-alike obligations are satisfied by this
file; the recolored SVG is distributed under the same license terms for the
artwork layer.
